Our Story

Born from Passion, Bottled with Care

Nick of Thyme was born in April 2020 when Nicky Godwin opened the doors of her dream shop in the heart of historic Downtown Wetumpka. What began as a passion for great food and exceptional ingredients has grown into one of Alabama's most beloved specialty food destinations.

We source our ultra-premium olive oils and aged balsamic vinegars from a trusted supplier in Oakland, California — ensuring every bottle that hits our shelves meets the highest standards of freshness, quality, and flavor.

"The fresher the olive oil, the better the flavor — we bottle ours right here and get it to you at peak freshness."
— Nicky Godwin, Founder
